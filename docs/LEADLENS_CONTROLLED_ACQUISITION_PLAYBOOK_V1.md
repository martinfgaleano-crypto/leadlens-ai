# LEADLENS — CONTROLLED ACQUISITION PLAYBOOK V1

**Status:** CANONICAL / READINESS-ONLY. HQ has **not** opened the acquisition gate. This document is the operating system for deliberately acquiring, qualifying, onboarding, observing and learning from the **first 5–10 real customers** once the gate opens. It is not an execution order. No contact, list-building, publishing, ads, product/Pricing/Billing changes are authorized by this document.

**Consumes (does not redefine):** category, ICP, envelope, one-time catalog, frozen Pricing matrix, product journey. **Related canonical artifacts:** `docs/PRICING_OPERATIONAL_ENTITLEMENT_MATRIX_V1.md`, `docs/FIRST_PILOT_RUNBOOK.md`, `docs/GO_TO_MARKET_MASTER_CHECKLIST.md`, `docs/strategy/RETENTION_ENGINE.md`, `docs/strategy/BETA_OPERATIONS_PLAYBOOK.md`, landing (`app/demo-pipeline/page.tsx`), public sample (`/sample`).

## PURPOSE
Convert LeadLens's now-closed product capability into **high-quality controlled commercial learning** from 5–10 first customers. Optimize for *learning* — do customers understand LeadLens, does it change/clarify account prioritization, does Evidence build trust, is Timing worth paying for, is HOLD understood, does Monitor create repeat value, does the workflow fit real behavior, does founder support stay bounded. **Do not optimize for users, reach, leads, followers, or volume.**

## CANONICAL BOUNDARIES
- **Category:** ACCOUNT OPPORTUNITY INTELLIGENCE. **Promise:** *Know which accounts deserve attention now.* **B2B is a launch wedge, not the category.**
- LeadLens is **not**: a contact database, Apollo replacement, lead scraper, email list, outreach automation, CRM, generic research, investment sourcing, recruiting, or procurement intelligence.
- **Decisions:** Prioritize / Validate / Monitor / Hold. **Dimensions:** Fit / Timing / Evidence. No HOT/WARM/COLD, no opaque score.
- **Frozen truth:** Intelligence V1, Customer Activation V1, Product Operability V1, Entitlements V1, Repeat Value/Monitor V1, HOLD Option B — all CLOSED. Landing FROZEN/launch-ready. Pricing V1 architecture FROZEN. **Billing/Lemon PAUSED** (merchant review). Product & Engineering stays quiet unless a genuine launch blocker appears.

## PRIMARY FIRST-CUSTOMER ARCHETYPE
**A specialized US B2B consultancy / professional-services firm selling high-value services into manufacturing, equipment, or operational companies.**
- 3–25 employees; founder/partner-led commercial motion.
- High-value engagements; account selection is economically meaningful.
- Does real research **before** outreach/BD.
- Target-account universe has meaningful public evidence.
- Decision-maker is accessible.
- Target market aligns with the **US manufacturing / equipment / operations** envelope (strongest current coverage).

## SECONDARY ARCHETYPE
**A founder-led / lean US B2B sales or BD team selling operational software or high-value operational services.** Use only in Customers 6–10 (controlled variation), after the primary pattern is validated.

## CUSTOMER QUALITY FILTER
**Prefer** where: account value makes prioritization matter; research precedes outreach; timing changes attention; buyer can articulate a commercial objective; buyer can judge whether LeadLens helped; public account evidence is discoverable; the account universe sits inside the validated envelope.
**Avoid / NOT YET** where: they only want contact emails; expect thousands of leads; success = email volume; want CRM/outreach automation; account economics are too low to justify prioritization; target market falls outside validated coverage; cannot articulate an objective; unwilling to give structured feedback.

## CONTROLLED OFFER
**FOUNDER-INVITED GUIDED INTELLIGENCE EVALUATION.** Not a new SKU, not a new price.
- **Access path (operable today):** existing **beta access is auth-only** (Customer Activation V1 — no entitlement gate, no payment required). A qualified, invited customer signs in and uses the real self-serve product. No product change needed.
- **During Lemon pause:** run the evaluation **free** (founder-invited), and capture **willingness-to-pay as directional signal only** (verbal/interview). Do **not** collect payment while Lemon is under merchant review.
- **When Lemon is live:** qualified customers convert on the **existing one-time catalog** — Preview $7 / Brief $25 / Intelligence $59 / Premium $129 — matched to their actual scope. No new tier.
- **Founder MAY:** invite, qualify, set expectations, observe onboarding, interview, handle exceptional issues.
- **Founder MUST NOT (as normal operation):** run Intelligence, edit/curate results or Evidence, fix routine reports, assign entitlement, trigger Monitor, or hand-deliver the product. Founder involvement in the *core loop* is a workflow-defect signal, not the offer.
- **DEPENDENCY:** whether/when to (a) charge, (b) formally comp, or (c) promise a specific price during the pause is an **HQ / Pricing / Billing decision** (see Domain Dependencies). Do not invent a payment workaround.

## CHANNEL HIERARCHY
Ranked for *5–10 customers* on ICP-reach × trust × speed × founder-effort × signal quality × learning density:
1. **PRIMARY — Warm founder-led introductions / founder network** (highest trust, highest learning density, lowest cost, perfect for a handful).
2. **SECONDARY — Manual, highly targeted founder-led LinkedIn outreach** (one-to-one, hand-picked; not automation).
3. Deferred (explicitly, not now): second-degree intros → targeted email → niche communities → partnerships → content → organic social → paid ads → marketplace exposure.
**Paid acquisition: WAIT. Broad cold automation: DEFER. Content machine: NOT REQUIRED for the first 10.**

## MESSAGE ARCHITECTURE
Preserve *Account Opportunity Intelligence* / *Know which accounts deserve attention now.* Emphasize attention allocation, Evidence, Timing, commercial context, account decisions, changing conditions (What Changed / Monitor). **Avoid:** "best leads", AI hype, guaranteed opportunities, buying-intent claims, automated prospecting, productivity exaggeration.

1. **One-line description:** "LeadLens tells you which accounts in your market deserve attention now — and shows the evidence behind each decision."
2. **Founder intro (spoken):** "I built LeadLens because deciding *which* accounts to pursue — and when — is where teams waste the most effort. It reads your commercial context, researches the organizations that matter, and returns a short, evidence-backed set of accounts worth your attention, each with a decision and what to validate. I'm inviting a few teams whose work depends on that judgment to try it and tell me where it's right and wrong."
3. **Warm-introduction / outreach (3–5 sentences):** "You do real research before you go after an account — that's exactly the judgment LeadLens is built to sharpen. It takes your commercial context, investigates the relevant organizations, and returns a small set of accounts that deserve attention now, each with the change behind it, the evidence, what's uncertain, and a decision. I'm running a small, hands-on evaluation with a few teams like yours and would value your read on where it helps and where it doesn't. No contact lists, no outreach automation — just clearer account decisions. Open to a short look?"
4. **Manual LinkedIn message:** "Hi [name] — you run real account research before BD, so I'd value your eye on something. LeadLens reads a commercial context and returns a short, evidence-backed set of accounts worth attention now, with a decision and what to validate for each. I'm doing a small guided evaluation — would you be open to trying it and telling me where it's useful vs not?"
5. **Landing → conversation bridge:** "You've seen the sample — the real thing runs on *your* commercial context. I'll set you up with access and walk through your first result together."
6. **First-call explanation (30–60s):** "LeadLens finds *account opportunities*, not guaranteed buyers. You'll get a small prioritized portfolio — Prioritize / Validate / Monitor / Hold — each with evidence and what to check. A HOLD is a real, useful answer: it can save you from chasing an account that isn't ready. Coverage is strongest in US manufacturing/equipment/operations today. A run takes a few minutes."
7. **Post-demo follow-up:** "Here's your access. Start at /activate, describe your commercial context, confirm the interpretation, and run it. Look at one account's full case — the change, the evidence, the limits, the decision. I'll follow up to hear what moved in your priorities and what you distrusted."
8. **Objection responses:**
   - *"I want emails/contacts."* → "LeadLens deliberately doesn't sell contacts — it decides *which accounts* deserve attention and why. If you need a contact database, we're not the right tool."
   - *"Is this AI hype?"* → "Every decision comes with its evidence, its limits, and what to validate — you inspect the reasoning, not a score."
   - *"Can it guarantee buyers?"* → "No — it flags where timing and evidence make an account worth your attention now; you still qualify and act."
   - *"Does it do outreach?"* → "No. It stops at the decision; you own the outreach."
   - *"Only a few accounts?"* → "Intentionally — a short, defensible set beats a long list you can't act on."

## QUALIFICATION
Deterministic rubric — **8 dimensions**, each scored **2 / 1 / 0**. Bands: **GOOD FIT ≥ 12 and no hard-reject**; **BORDERLINE 8–11**; **NOT YET ≤ 7 or any hard-reject**.

| # | Dimension | 2 (strong) | 1 (partial) | 0 / hard-reject |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account economics | High-value engagements; one right account matters | Mid-value | Low value / volume game **(reject)** |
| 2 | Research intensity | Researches before outreach/BD | Sometimes | Never; wants a list **(reject)** |
| 3 | Commercial-objective clarity | Can state objective + who matters | Rough | Cannot articulate **(reject)** |
| 4 | Timing sensitivity | Timing changes who they pursue | Somewhat | Irrelevant |
| 5 | Public evidence availability | Targets have real public footprint | Mixed | Opaque targets |
| 6 | Intelligence-envelope fit | US mfg/equipment/logistics/ops | US operational software/services (guided) | Outside coverage **(reject)** |
| 7 | Access to actual user/decision-maker | The buyer/user will engage directly | Via proxy | No access |

**Hard-reject / NOT YET (any one):** wants contact lists · needs bulk lead volume · outside supported envelope · account value too low · cannot articulate objective · unwilling to evaluate · wants outreach automation.

## EXPECTATION SETTING
Send/say **before** they judge results (reduces false-negative perception):
- LeadLens identifies **account opportunities, not guaranteed buyers**.
- **PRIORITIZE ≠ purchase guarantee.** **HOLD is a valid, useful result** — absence of a current opportunity can prevent wasted effort.
- **Evidence quality varies** with an account's public footprint.
- **Coverage is bounded** today (strongest: US manufacturing/equipment/operations; Colombia guided). Not universal.
- A run **takes several minutes** — not instant.
- **Monitor is periodic re-evaluation, not real-time streaming.**
- LeadLens **does not deliver contact lists** and **does not perform outreach**.

## FOUNDER OPERATING CADENCE
Minimum, learning-generating, non-hiding:
- **Per prospect:** 1 warm intro → 1 qualification pass → 1 first call (context + expectations).
- **After yes:** invite (beta access), then **observe onboarding without touching the loop**.
- **After first result:** Interview #1 (post-result).
- **After return / ~2–3 weeks:** Interview #2 (repeat-use).
- **Exceptional only:** unblock a genuine defect (log it, route it — do not normalize it).
**Forbidden as normal op:** running Intelligence, editing results, Evidence curation, Monitor triggering, entitlement assignment, manual delivery. If the founder must do any of these to make a customer succeed, that is a **workflow defect to fix**, not a task to repeat.

## POST-RESULT INTERVIEW
(≤10 questions; skip any that don't apply)
1. What decision were you trying to make?
2. What did you expect LeadLens to tell you?
3. What changed after seeing the output?
4. Did any account move up or down in priority? Which, and why?
5. What did you distrust?
6. Which piece of Evidence mattered most?
7. What was missing?
8. Was HOLD understandable and useful here?
9. What would you have done without LeadLens?
10. Would you pay for this at the catalog price? (directional)

## REPEAT-USE INTERVIEW
(≤8 questions)
1. What brought you back (or why didn't you return)?
2. Did anything material change on an account since last time — did LeadLens show it?
3. Was "What Changed / no material change" clear and trustworthy?
4. Did Monitor/re-review save you effort or surface something new?
5. Did a decision evolve (e.g., Hold → something)? Was that credible?
6. Where did the workflow feel unnatural on return?
7. What would make this a standing part of your process?
8. Would you pay again / subscribe when available? (directional)

## CUSTOMERS 1–2 — DIAGNOSTIC VALIDATION
- **Objective:** Does the core workflow create understandable, real value **without hidden founder operation**?
- **Permitted intervention:** onboarding observation + interviews + exceptional-defect unblock (logged).
- **Metrics:** #1–#6, #9.
- **Failure signals:** founder had to run/curate Intelligence; customer couldn't explain the result; no decision impact.
- **Gate to 3–5:** both customers activated self-serve, ≥1 clear decision-impact story, **zero founder core-loop operation**, no RED condition.

## CUSTOMERS 3–5 — REPEATABILITY VALIDATION
- **Objective:** Does that value **repeat without higher** founder intervention?
- **Permitted intervention:** *less* than 1–2 (declining).
- **Metrics:** #1–#7, #9, #10.
- **Failure signals:** rising founder involvement; repeated misunderstanding of Decision/HOLD; repeated Evidence distrust.
- **Gate to 6–10:** ≥3 activated self-serve, decision-impact repeats, founder core-loop ops still 0, ≥1 genuine return/Monitor signal, support burden flat/declining.

## CUSTOMERS 6–10 — CONTROLLED VARIATION
- **Objective:** Can the validated pattern extend carefully to the **secondary archetype / bounded variation** (e.g., operational software/services, or a second envelope segment)?
- **Permitted intervention:** minimal; variation is in *archetype*, not in founder effort.
- **Metrics:** full set; watch #4, #7, #8 for the new archetype.
- **Failure signals:** the pattern only worked for the primary archetype; envelope mismatch; WTP collapses off-archetype.
- **Gate to "Controlled Acquisition Ready to widen":** ≥2 successful secondary-archetype customers with comparable value + bounded founder effort.

**No automatic 2 → 5 → 10 progression — each gate is an explicit HQ decision.**

## STAGE GATES (summary)
`1–2` pass → `3–5`; `3–5` pass → `6–10`; `6–10` pass → HQ decides whether to widen (channel/envelope/paid). Any RED at any stage → pause all acquisition.

## ACQUISITION STOP CONDITIONS
- **RED (pause immediately):** privacy/security issue · customer-data exposure · repeated *materially wrong* account intelligence · activation broken · systemic checkout/entitlement failure · founder must manually produce intelligence to make customers succeed.
- **AMBER (diagnose before adding customers):** repeated misunderstanding of Decision/HOLD · repeated high-fit-but-irrelevant accounts · repeated Evidence distrust · repeated founder intervention · repeated wrong geography · Monitor value unclear.
- **GREEN:** normal learning.
- **Do NOT pause for:** one HOLD · one irrelevant account · one feature request · one user who dislikes it.
