## Product answer first
On the same frozen customer + 7 fresh neutral companies: LeadLens produced the most *defensible*
decisions (5 validate / 2 hold, SEC-grounded triggers, honest "mechanism/access not established",
validate-first actions). Claude+Vibe produced the most *immediately act-on-able* intelligence
(dated, priced 2026 deals in one call + named decision-makers). Claude-alone was coherent but
largely unverified. The ideal for a buyer is LeadLens's discipline **plus** Vibe's access — neither
arm delivers both.

judge are impossible single-agent. Scores are directional only. The neutral 7-company universe was
fixed before any arm ran and excludes every company LeadLens researched in development.

## Reproducibility (strategic)

(Vibe events) + contacts (Vibe) + decent reasoning (Claude). LeadLens's edge is consistency,
honest abstention, counterevidence and repeatability WITHOUT prompt engineering.
Differentiation type: **REASONING / DECISION-SYSTEM + WORKFLOW + TRUST** — NOT a data moat.

differentiation is narrow (decision discipline + honesty + repeatability) and reproducible by a
skilled user. Whether that is worth paying for is the exact question for real buyers.
