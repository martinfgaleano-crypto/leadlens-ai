# External Blind Buyer Scorecard (for 6–8 real BD / sales-leadership / consulting-founder evaluators)

You will see three anonymized outputs (OUTPUT X / Y / Z) answering: *"which of these 7 companies deserve our commercial attention now for our post-merger-integration consulting service, why, and what to do next."* ~5–10 min.

1. Which output would you KEEP if you could keep only one? (X / Y / Z)
2. Which most clearly tells you WHERE to spend commercial effort?
3. Which do you TRUST most?
4. Which best tells you whether to ACT NOW or WAIT?
5. Which gives the most useful NEXT ACTION?
6. Which WASTES the least of your time?
7. What is MISSING from the one you preferred?
8. Would you PAY for the preferred output? (No / Low self-serve / $59+ / $300+ guided)
9. Would you want this REPEATEDLY (another market / client / month)?
10. Would you use it INSTEAD of your current research workflow? Why?
11. Does "do not pursue this one yet" (abstention) read as VALUABLE or as "it found nothing"?
12. Would you rather have a rigorous "validate X first" with no contact, or named contacts with lighter reasoning?

Success gate (stated preference only): LeadLens preferred ≥5/8 = positive signal · 4/8 = ambiguous · ≤3/8 = differentiation fails. Report raw counts. No fabricated votes.
